Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Infinity Digital HQ (infinitydigitalhq.com) is a website of Infinity Digital Development, Scott Smith’s studio in Denver, Colorado. The studio’s main site is infinitydigitaldev.com. This policy covers visits to this domain and business email sent from it.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It has no account login, no contact form, and no advertising or analytics scripts of our own. We do not set cookies from this site.

GitHub Pages hosts the files. Like most hosts, GitHub may record standard connection logs — for example an IP address, browser type, and the page requested — to run and protect the service. Those logs are covered by GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email from this domain

Infinity Digital Development sends business email from infinitydigitalhq.com about AI-powered websites and automation for small businesses. A message is addressed to someone at a company we believe may want that help.

Those messages name Infinity Digital Development, say why we are writing, and include a way to reach us and a way to opt out. If an email brought you here, it was sent by the studio.

Information we use

For outreach we may keep a business contact’s name, role, company, email address, and a short note about why we wrote. We take that from public business information and from what you send back.

If you reply to an email, call 720-252-7037, or write through the contact page on the main site, we keep the correspondence so we can answer and, if you hire the studio, do the work.

How we use it

  • To send and reply to messages about the studio’s services.
  • To remember who asked us not to email them.
  • To run a project you have asked us to do.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Opting out

You can stop marketing email from this domain at any time:

  • Reply to the message and ask to unsubscribe.
  • Call 720-252-7037.
  • Write through infinitydigitaldev.com/contact.

We honor opt-out requests. After we process one, we will not send further marketing email to that address. Mail you ask us to send, such as a reply about a project, is separate from marketing email.

How long we keep it

Outreach contacts stay only while we are using them for relevant business email. We keep a record of opt-outs so we do not write again. Project correspondence is kept as long as we need it for the work and for ordinary business records.

Children

This site and our emails are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the page and the date above.
